About VANTA

Born in Miami. Built for the global creative industry.

VANTA was founded in Miami five years ago with a clear purpose: to make the casting process more efficient, more intentional and better aligned with the creative vision behind every campaign.

What began with a simple observation — that brands and agencies were spending too much time searching through endless talent lists — became a specialized casting and creative production company built around finding the right person for the right project.

Today, VANTA works across fashion, swimwear, intimate apparel, beauty, skincare, lifestyle, advertising and commercial production. We partner with brands, advertising agencies, production companies, photographers, creative directors and other creative teams to source, curate, cast, coordinate and book talent.

Our approach combines a strong understanding of talent with organized production support. We don't simply provide a list of names. We take the brief, understand the creative direction, identify the right talent, manage the casting process and help move the project from initial request to confirmed booking and production.

Based in Miami and connected to a broader creative network, VANTA is built for teams that value speed, precision, professionalism and talent that genuinely fits the vision.

VANTA FACT: Miami was the starting point. The creative network was always meant to be much bigger.
